# Assault tactics, jeeps and grenades

Stage 2 retains the 18/24/30 infantry waves. The gun now overheats after 35 continuous rounds (about 4.3 seconds at eight rounds per second), down from 40. Cooling is 28 heat units per second, with release at 30: a full overheat locks firing for 2.5 seconds. Short bursts avoid that lockout.

- One in three regular infantry carries two smoke grenades. Half of these carriers screen the landing and wire crossings; the others screen the wire crossings and final approach. Each pauses for a 1.1-second throw and lobs a canister seven to ten meters ahead. Each assigned position is used only once. Killing the thrower before release stops deployment; a released canister survives its thrower. The screen blooms after a 1.1-second flight, drifts downwind and dissipates over 12 seconds. Smoke obscures sight but never blocks bullets or damages the bunker.
- Two thirds of regular riflemen use foxholes and crawl at 60% speed near cover. Five larger sandbagged positions have 1.65-meter depressions, contrasting excavated earth, raised rims and timber revetments. Occupants stay 9–13.5 seconds, duck, peek and fire; some throw grenades. Suppression delays their exit up to a 15-second limit. Bullets hit exposed troops, while rifle grenades and strafing runs reach concealed occupants. Nearby squads rally for the exit rush.
- Sand ridges and a central drainage channel add elevation. Terrain blocks bullets; clear gaps provide firing lanes. Squads alternate advancing and providing covering fire on a three-second cycle. Smoke throws gather nearby soldiers for a coordinated push.
- Each wave includes a machine-gun team (4.5-second setup, 3 damage every five seconds); waves two and three also include a mortar crew (seven-second setup, a shell every eight seconds). Equipment, a second crew member, setup animations, tracers and mortar launches identify priority targets without floating labels. Killing the crew stops future attacks; airborne shells still land.
- Landing craft have two-hit gunners and eight-hit ramps. Silencing a gunner stops its covering fire; breaking a ramp jams that craft for seven seconds. Other boats can continue unloading and the damaged ramp eventually opens.

- One/two/three jeeps per wave, first arriving five seconds in and subsequent vehicles seven seconds apart. They drive at 9 m/s through the steel, concrete and wire gaps, unload four soldiers at 38 m, and reverse out along the same route.
- Each jeep requires 18 hits. Destroying a loaded vehicle denies its remaining passengers and awards 400 points. Armor is not displayed during play; vehicle hits produce sparks rather than blood, and wrecks smolder.
- Infantry within 42 m stop and telegraph a grenade throw for 1.4 seconds. Killing the thrower before release cancels it. An airborne grenade continues even if the thrower dies and deals 12 bunker damage after 2.2 seconds. Each soldier carries one grenade.
- Grenade throws are telegraphed by the soldier animation, without floating warnings. Grenades follow visible ballistic arcs to the ground directly outside the firing position. Hits produce a brief blast flash and warm light, flying debris, a sharp decaying camera jolt, and six seconds of drifting dust and smoke. Reduce camera motion disables the jolt and roll. Blast overlap is capped at four; pause freezes the effects and retry clears them.
- **G / Grenade button:** three rifle grenades per wave, 115-meter range, 1.7-second launch cooldown, 1.35-second arc and seven-meter blast radius. They clear troops in cover and deal 12 damage to jeeps.
- **V / Air support button:** earn a strafing run with 12 combat credits (one per infantry kill, four per destroyed jeep). Store one charge; aim before calling it. A visible aircraft attacks a 48-meter line across the beach after a two-second approach. Explosions and tracers follow its pass; the strike finishes after five seconds.
- After waves one and two, choose one benefit: repair up to 35 bunker integrity, upgrade the barrel (10% less heat per shot and +4 cooling per second per level, maximum two), or start with five rifle grenades. Every choice cools the gun and replenishes at least three grenades. Combat freezes while choosing.
- Wave completion waits for scheduled jeeps, remaining infantry, airborne projectiles and active air support. Pause freezes threat timers. Retry clears troops, vehicles, effects and projectiles.
- Rendering is bounded at 48 active infantry, 18 corpses and six vehicles across the encounter. Vehicle geometry/materials are shared and disposed on stage exit.

Validation covers full three-wave victory, heat recovery, transport durability, reinforcement counts, crawling and foxhole obstacle clearance, interruptible throws, smoke lifetime and pass-through fire, committed airborne damage, wave completion, pause, retry and graphics-resource cleanup.
